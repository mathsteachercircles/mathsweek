About Maths Week Australia

What is Maths Week Australia, and when is it?

Maths Week Australia is a free national celebration of maths for K–12 schools, running 10–14 May 2027. It gives schools a dedicated time in the calendar to come together around maths, and puts a national spotlight on the great work happening in schools and communities across the country.

Who is Maths Week Australia for?

Everyone! 2027 will be the first year of Maths Week Australia, and our focus is on providing resources and support for K–12 teachers.

However, if you're a parent, early childhood educator or part of a non-school organisation, you're also very welcome to celebrate Maths Week Australia in your own way during 10–14 May 2027. Teachers outside Australia are welcome too. Join us as honorary Aussies for the week!

Is it a competition?

No. Maths Week Australia is a celebration, not a competition. Our resources focus on mathematical thinking, creativity and working together rather than speed, scores or top results. The aim is to help a wider range of students see that maths is for them.

Is it free?

Yes. Registering, the resources and the live guest speaker events are all free.

For teachers who want to go further, there will be optional paid extras. You don't need these to take part.

Who is behind Maths Week Australia?

Maths Week Australia is an initiative of Maths Teacher Circles, a for-profit, for-purpose company. Maths Teacher Circles provides both free and paid professional learning and resources for thousands of K–12 teachers across Australia, and recognised the need for an event like this to exist.

The week is backed by our Community Ambassadors, as well as maths organisations and associations across the country. Meet them here.

Taking part as a teacher

Do I need to register?

You don't need to register to take part in Maths Week Australia. But by registering, you'll receive free resources we've developed specifically for the event. You'll also be invited to free guest speaker events and go into the draw to win prizes for your classroom and school.

Register for free here.

Do we have to use your resources or do specific activities?

No. You're welcome to celebrate Maths Week Australia however you like, using whichever resources suit your students.

We recognise that every school is different. Most schools run Maths Week through classroom activities, lunchtime events, school-wide maths festivals and/or Family Maths Nights. The free K–12 resources we're putting together are designed to work in all of these settings.

Whatever your context, our aim is to give you the resources and support to make Maths Week Australia a success for you and your students. Register here to get them.

How much time does it take?

As much or as little as you like. One lesson with one class is a great way to take part, and some schools will be running activities across the whole week.

Our resources are designed to be simple to run and need no special materials, so you can pick what fits your timetable.

Does it have to be a whole-school thing?

No. While many schools will celebrate Maths Week Australia school-wide, not everyone is in a position to do so.

At its heart, Maths Week Australia is about bringing people together to enjoy, do and think about maths. All it takes is one teacher and one group of students to get started!

Can we run it in a different week?

Maths Week Australia is officially taking place 10–14 May 2027. These are the dates to put in your calendar if you would like to be part of this celebration that's happening nation-wide. During the week, there'll be updates about what other schools are doing across the country plus live guest speaker events.

If that week doesn't work for your school, you can run it at a time that suits you. The free resources are yours to use whenever you like.

Other ways to get involved

Do I have to be part of a school to take part?

No. A core aim of Maths Week Australia is to put a national spotlight on maths that is positive, and we recognise that maths is learned (and done) in many places outside of schools. Wherever you are, you're welcome to celebrate in your own way during 10–14 May 2027.

I'm a parent. How can I get involved?

There are two ways you can support your child as part of Maths Week Australia:

  1. Speak to your child's school and encourage them to register for Maths Week Australia.
  2. Run maths-based activities with your child during the week (10–14 May 2027).

A core aim of Maths Week Australia is to help a wider range of students see that maths is for them. Your support can make a meaningful difference!

I work for a STEM organisation. How can we get involved?

Great! There are two ways you can support Maths Week Australia:

  1. Sponsor this nationwide event to help us reach more students across Australia. Contact us for sponsorship details.
  2. Run your own activities and events in your workplace or community during Maths Week Australia (10–14 May 2027).
